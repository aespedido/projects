## Insights
**1. Furniture products dominate top-performing products in profit, followed by Technology**
The top-performing products in profit are largely from the Furniture category, highlighting its role as a key driver in profitability. Overall, Furniture generated a profit of ~$81K, followed by Technology at ~$48K. In contrast, Office Supplies only generated ~$3K in profit, indicating a clear gap in performance across categories.

**2. Clothing & Assessories delivers higher profit margins despite lower sales**
The Clothing & Assessories category has lower sales (~$69K) and profit (~$26k) however it achieves higher profit margins of around 37%, compared to Furniture (31%) and Technology (34%). This indicates that while it contributes less in overall revenue, it is efficient in generating profit, suggesting an opportunity to grow this category. 

**3. Discounts are inefficient in driving profitability**
Profit margins were found to decline steadily as discounts increased, indicating discounts had a negative impact on proftability. This suggests that discounts should be carefully managed.
