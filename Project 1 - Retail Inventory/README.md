# Shopping Trends Inventory Analysis

## Introduction
This project simulates a scenario of a retail business looking to optimise its inventory management by identifying which products to stock more or less of. Sales data was analysed to assess product sales performance based on seasonality, product category and customer preferences.

The analysis explores:
- Seasonal trends in product sales
- High and low performing products
- Customer preferences in colour and size

As part of this analysis, an interactive Excel dashboard was built to visualise these trends and patterns. Users are able to filter by season, category and specific items to uncover deeper insights.

Ultimately this analysis will help the retail business make more informed decisions with their inventory. The business can ensure popular products are readily available for their customers while minimising excess inventory of their underperforming products. 

## Dashboard Approach and Design
The first step of this project was to uncover a high level view of seasonal sales and product performance. This was done by creating pivot tables to find units sold by season and the top 10 popular items and top 5 least popular items. Next, pivot tables were created to find product sales based on season, sales by product colour and sales by product size. Following this analysis, visualisations were created to form the dashboard which included:

- Bar chart - Sales performance by season: Provides a high-level overview of sales across the seasons
- Heatmap - Seasonal item performance: Highlights product performance across different seasons
- Bar Charts - Top 10 popular items and Top 5 least popular items: Helps identify high and low performing items
- Bar charts - Colour and Size preferences: Show customer demand for certain colours and sizes

The top 10 popular items and top 5 least popular items bar charts were paired with slicers for season and category to find high and low performing items based on those variables. Slicers for season, category and item were also attached to the colour preferences and size preferences bar charts. Therefore allowing for deeper analysis such as finding colour preferences for specific products 

## Insights & Reccommendations
Based on the analysis of product sales across seasons, categories and customer preferences, the following key insights were identified:

### Overall seasonal performance
The sales performance per season was assessed to determine if certain seasons performed better than others and whether that would indicate that overall stocks levels needed to be higher for high performing seasons and lower for low performing seasons. It was found that there is only a 4.6% difference between the highest performing month (Spring) and the lowest performing month (Summer), therefore concluding that stock levels didn't need to drastically change based on the time of year.

### Product performance
The Top 10 popular products was identified to be:
1. Jewelry
2. Blouse
3. Pants
4. Shirt
5. Dress
6. Sweater
7. Jacket
8. Belt
9. Sunglasses
10. Coat
