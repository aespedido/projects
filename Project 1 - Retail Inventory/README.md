# Shopping Trends Inventory Analysis

## Introduction
This project simulates a scenario of a retail business looking to optimise its inventory management by identifying which products to stock more or less of. Sales data was analysed to identify high and low performing items based on seasonality, product category and customer preferences.

The analysis explores:
- Seasonal trends in product sales
- High and low performing products
- Customer preferences in colour and size

As part of this analysis, a comprehensive interactive Excel dashboard was built to visualise these trends. Users are able to filter by season, category and specific items to uncover deeper insights.

## Dashboard Approach and Design
The first step of this project was to uncover a high level view of seasonal sales and product performance by creating pivot tables to find units sold by season and the top 10 popular items and top 5 least popular items. Then pivot tables were created to find item sales based on season, sales by item colour and sales by item size. Following this analysis, visualisations were created to form the dashboard which included:

- Bar chart - Sales performance by season: Provides a high-level overview of sales across the seasons
- Heatmap - Seasonal item performance: Highlights item performance across different seasons
- Bar Charts - Top 10 popular items and Top 5 least popular items: Helps identify high and low performing items
- Bar charts - Colour and Size preferences: Show customer demand for certain colours and sizes

The top 10 popular items and top 5 least popular items bar charts were paired with slicers for season and category to find high and low performing items based on those variables. Slicers for season, category and item were also attached to the colour preferences and size preferences bar charts. Therefore allowing for deeper analysis such as finding colour preferences for specific items.
