# Shopping Trends Inventory Analysis

This project simulates a scenario of a retail business looking to optimise its inventory management. A dataset of the business' sales was analysed to identify high and low performing items to determine what to stock more or less of.

The analysis explores:
- Seasonal trends in product sales
- High and low performing products
- Customer preferences in colour and size

As part of this analysis, a comprehensive interactive Excel dashboard was built to visualise these trends. Users are able to filter by season, category and specific items to uncover deeper insights.
